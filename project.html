Maji Ndogo Water Scarcity & Access

Goal: Assess regional water-service coverage gaps, analyze queue times, and identify crime patterns to support targeted public interventions.
Key Insights: Shared taps serve the largest population; rural regions account for roughly 64% of people served. Queue times peaked drastically on Saturdays, with Kilimani having the highest total queue time. Concurrently, data showed females represented 64% of crime victims (prominently harassment/sexual assault), with incidents heavily spiking during evening/night hours.
Strategic Recommendations: Increase water supply capacity and construct extra localized water points during peak hours. Prioritize awareness/protection programs for vulnerable groups and allocate tactical policing to high-crime provinces during peak night intervals.
